01 / About

Background

I am an Associate Professor in the Kageyama Group at Kyoto University’s Graduate School of Engineering.

My research focuses on crystalline solids and their structure–property relationships, with particular interests in ferroelectricity, multiferroic behaviour, negative thermal expansion and batteries.

I obtained my undergraduate degrees from Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University and the University of Liverpool in 2016. At Oxford, I worked with Professor Michael Hayward on topochemical synthesis and the crystal symmetries of layered perovskite oxides with polar and multiferroic properties, completing my DPhil in Inorganic Chemistry in 2020.

I then joined Professor Hiroshi Kageyama’s group in Kyoto, first as a postdoctoral researcher and later as an Assistant Professor. I was promoted to a permanent Associate Professor position in March 2025.

My doctoral work received the Malvern Panalytical Thesis Prize for Physical Crystallography.
